Security & Data Protection

TLS 1.3 Encryption

All transit data between your browser, our API backend, and Microsoft/Google servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3 with AES-256-GCM cipher suites.

OAuth 2.0 Credentials Isolation

We authenticate using secure delegated OAuth 2.0 authorization codes. WorkSphere AI never accesses, collects, or stores your primary passwords.

Zero Token Retention

API access tokens are held in short-lived encrypted in-memory cookies or secure storage. Logging out completely destroys all local sessions and token registries.

Private Processing

Your email content, file metadata, and meeting notes are processed in real-time in-memory. No workspace text is ever saved to disk or utilized for training public LLM models.

Enterprise Support & Compliance

Dedicated SLA & Support

Enterprise subscribers receive 24/7 dedicated support with a guaranteed response time SLA of under 30 minutes from our technical operations team.

Self-Host / Private Tenant

WorkSphere AI supports on-premise Kubernetes hosting or single-tenant virtual private cloud (VPC) deployments in your own Azure, GCP, or AWS subscription.
